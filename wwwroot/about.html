About Path & Paws

Personal care. Professional standards.

Hi, I'm Robin, founder of Path & Paws.

I created Path & Paws because I believe dogs deserve more than simply being taken for a walk. For many dogs, busy group walks can be overwhelming, while others simply benefit from having more individual attention.

That's why I provide one-to-one dog walking, giving every dog the time, attention and care they deserve.

Every dog is different. Their walks are tailored around their personality, routine, energy levels and comfort, rather than expecting them to fit into someone else's schedule.

Care you can trust

My aim is simple: to provide calm, reliable and professional care that gives you complete peace of mind, while giving your dog a walking experience they'll genuinely enjoy.

Your dog isn't just another booking in a diary. When they're in my care, I take responsibility for their safety, wellbeing and enjoyment seriously.

Professional, responsible care

Path & Paws is fully insured, I am trained in dog first aid, and I am a member of the MDWA.

One dog. One walk. One-to-one attention.
