Cookie Policy

How we use cookies

Path & Paws uses a small amount of storage technology to make the website work and to remember your cookie preference.

Essential storage

The website uses essential storage to remember whether you have made a cookie choice. This is used only to remember your preference and does not track you across websites.

Analytics and advertising

We currently do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies on this website.

Managing your choice

You can control cookies and similar technologies through your browser settings. If we introduce non-essential cookies or tracking technologies in the future, we will update this policy and request consent where required.

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact Path & Paws.
