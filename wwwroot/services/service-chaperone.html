Your Dog. Your Wedding. No Worries.

Because your dog is part of the family, why shouldn't they be part of your big day?

Our Wedding Dog Chaperone service allows your four-legged family member to share in your wedding celebrations while we take care of everything else.

We Bring Your Dog to the Wedding

From getting ready in the morning to photographs, the ceremony and celebrations afterwards, we provide dedicated one-to-one care for your dog throughout their time at your wedding.

We take responsibility for the practicalities, so you can concentrate on enjoying your big day.

Whether your dog is walking down the aisle, appearing in your wedding photographs or simply enjoying being nearby, we'll make sure they are comfortable, safe and cared for throughout the day.

What Does a Wedding Dog Chaperone Do?

Your dog's day is planned around their needs and your schedule. Depending on your requirements, our service can include:

  • Collection and transport – collecting your dog from home and transporting them safely to the wedding venue.
  • Grooming – taking your dog to the groomers so they too can be pampered and look their best.
  • Getting ready together – bringing your dog to join you while you prepare for the ceremony, where the venue and arrangements allow.
  • Ceremony support – accompanying your dog if they have a role in the ceremony, including walking down the aisle or being part of the celebrations.
  • Wedding photographs – helping make sure your dog is available for those important photographs without you having to organise them.
  • Exercise and toilet breaks – providing appropriate walks, toilet breaks and time away from the excitement when your dog needs it.
  • Quiet time – giving your dog a calm place to rest when the excitement becomes too much.
  • End-of-day care – looking after your dog until the agreed end time and returning them home or to wherever they are going to spend the next few days
