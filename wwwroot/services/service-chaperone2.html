Your Dog. Your Wedding. No Worries.

Because your dog is part of the family, why shouldn't they be part of your big day?

Your Wedding, But Their Needs Come First

Weddings can be exciting, noisy and unpredictable. Even the most confident dog can become overwhelmed by crowds, music, unfamiliar surroundings and lots of new people.

That's why we don't simply keep your dog on a lead and follow you around all day.

We pay attention to their behaviour and give them the space, exercise, reassurance and quiet time they need to enjoy the experience too.

If your dog has had enough, we'll recognise it and take them somewhere quieter. Your dog doesn't have to stay involved simply because the wedding is still going on.

Be Part of the Memories

Imagine looking back at your wedding photographs and seeing your dog sitting beside you, walking down the aisle or sharing those special moments with you.

We'll help make those moments possible while taking responsibility for everything happening around them.

You can concentrate on the photographer, your guests and each other. We'll concentrate on your dog.
