Dog Walking That Puts Your Dog First

Perfect for Dogs Who Prefer Their Own Space

At PathAndPaws, we believe a dog walk should be more than simply getting out of the house.

It should be time to explore, sniff, run, play and enjoy being a dog.

That's why we offer personalised, one-to-one dog walking, giving your dog our full attention rather than treating them as one of a group.

One Dog. One Walker. Their Walk.

Every dog is different, Some love a fast-paced adventure. Others would rather take their time, investigate every scent and enjoy a quieter wander. Some need plenty of exercise; others simply benefit from getting outside and having some company.

Our walks are tailored around your dog's personality, age, fitness and individual needs.

No crowded group walks. No conveyor-belt approach. Just dedicated time with an experienced dog walker who gets to know your dog.
